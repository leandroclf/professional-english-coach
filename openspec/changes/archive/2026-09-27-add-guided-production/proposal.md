# Add a guided production step

## Why

The current path moves directly from a multiple-choice recognition check to an extended response. Add a short, contextualized sentence-building activity to bridge recognition and open production in each track.

## Scope

- Add one track-specific, one-sentence scaffold after the true/false and multiple-choice checks.
- Keep the authored open prompts, opt-in AI exchange, and reflection after the scaffold.
- Preserve existing in-progress session plans and local-only storage behavior.
- Explain in product documentation that this progression is a research-informed design hypothesis, not a proven outcome for this app.

## Out of scope

- Automated grading or proficiency/CEFR claims.
- Changes to spacing intervals, account/sync behavior, AI availability, or human tutoring.
