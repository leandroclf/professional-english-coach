# Introduce progressive exercise formats

## Why

The current tracks open with long-form writing. That asks a new learner to produce extended answers before they have warmed up with lower-pressure practice. A gradual sequence can reduce the initial effort and build toward professional communication.

## Scope

- Start each existing track with one true-or-false English usage check and one multiple-choice professional English exercise.
- Show correctness, the expected answer when needed, and a short explanation immediately after each closed-answer exercise.
- Continue into the existing open-ended professional prompts.
- Offer a consent-based AI conversation turn only after the learner completes the open-ended prompts, where the configured service is available.
- Keep learner answers local unless the learner explicitly opts into AI feedback.
- Record human conversation practice as a future product stage; do not represent it as an existing feature.

## Out of scope

- Formal placement, CEFR claims, proficiency scores, or claims that the sequence guarantees learning outcomes.
- A live human tutor marketplace or real-time human conversation feature.
- A continuously streaming AI voice conversation. The existing optional provider interaction remains text-based and returns one follow-up prompt and response.

## Acceptance

- Every track begins in order with a true-or-false exercise followed by a multiple-choice exercise, then proceeds to open responses.
- Closed-answer choices are keyboard-operable and required before continuing.
- The app records each selected option, reports whether it is correct, and explains the answer without describing this as a proficiency score.
- AI conversation is not offered during the guided exercises or open-ended prompts. After those prompts, learners can opt in to one final AI conversation turn when the configured service is available.
- Existing local sessions remain readable and resumable.
- English and PT-BR interface labels support the new exercise formats while exercise content stays in English.
- Automated checks pass; browser visual and keyboard acceptance is recorded separately where not available.
