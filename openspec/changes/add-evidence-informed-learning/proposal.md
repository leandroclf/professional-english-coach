# Evidence-informed learning paths

## Why
The three original professional tracks reuse one introductory lesson. Learners need varied input, beginner-length sessions, feedback they can inspect, delayed retrieval and an actionable next step. Competitor research is recorded in docs/competitive-analysis.md; academic sources in docs/research.md. Product features are inspiration, not efficacy evidence.

## Scope
Ship eight original bilingual micro-lessons; selectable recognition/guided/applied paths; original audio for every lesson; one captioned instructional video; ordering and listening completion; model comparison, self-explanation and scripted role rehearsal; contextual vocabulary capture; due/error-based recommendations; mixed retrieval; transparent activity metrics; weekly goals and persistent drafts. Preserve existing professional sessions, AI consent and local-only learner storage.

## Boundaries
No invented proficiency score, speech diagnosis, scientific validation of this app, cloud account, human tutor or live AI on the static host. Further suggestions are explicitly proposed in the roadmap. No copied proprietary exercises or licensed competitor media.
